Can ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini tell whether a song was made by AI?

AI generators like Suno now make songs most people can't tell from human ones. People already upload songs to chatbots and ask "is this AI?", and publishers and platforms need to know what they are hosting. Nobody has checked whether those answers mean anything.

Short answer

No. Not from the sound. They only "detect" AI when the file's tags say so or the lyrics give it away.

  • "Detection" is reading the label. ChatGPT and Claude open the file with code and find made with suno. Remove the tags and they drop straight to chance, answering "AI" for almost everything.
  • Gemini is the only one that listens, and it scores about 6/10 at every level, leaning "human" with stock phrases like "clean transients, natural groove".
  • Experiment 4 looked like a breakthrough: Gemini ranked every AI song above every human one. But it cited the lyrics each time, and they were AI-written. With human lyrics (experiment 5) it fell to chance.
  • ChatGPT sounds as if it listens but doesn't: it said "no vocals" on 7 of 10 vocal songs.
  • High frequencies didn't help: a human song at full quality was still called AI by all three (side test).

A pilot on the same 10 songs had already tried the experiment-3 condition in single-turn form, including full songs and ChatGPT's thinking mode. Everything landed at chance, which led to this graded design.

The two-turn chat

Turn 1 · describe

AI is not mentioned. Tempo, instruments, vocals, length, sample rate, tags, and how the model accessed the audio.

  accessed: "listened directly"
  length: 156 s   (actual 248 s)
  tags: null
Turn 2 · decide

Same chat: "AI" or "human", confidence 50–100%, main basis, up to five reasons.

Same song:
  HUMAN, 88%, "musical content"
  "authentic groove, natural humanization"
ChatGPT on it: AI, 99%, "made with suno" tag

The apps hide token counts, so tokens are estimated from page text (~4 characters per token). Gemini tokenises the audio itself (32 tokens per second), so full songs cost more than clips. Cost uses one illustrative rate ($3 / $15 per million tokens in / out), not any provider's prices.

Caveats

Small10 songs per experiment, one run each. A smoke test, not a precise measurement.
Consumer appsThe app picks the model version, and it can change without notice.
Our processing shows upIn experiment 3, Claude cited the low-pass and fades our clean-up adds to every clip. They are identical for AI and human, so they can't help, but they distort the reasoning.
Experiments 4–5All audio is 16 kHz mono; experiment 5 is all Suno (the dataset has no Udio songs with human lyrics).

Reproduce

  • Every answer, both turns: results/*_raw_log.jsonl. Full audio and answer keys are not in the repository; only the four excerpts above are.
